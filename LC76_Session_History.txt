MULTI-DAY STAY BLOCK BUILT (LiFePO4 Calculator). Four inputs — days at stop
1–14 · arrival SOC · sun scenario (good 5.5 h / average 4.0 h / poor 2.5 h
peak-sun-hours) · recharge-drive length (default 3 h). Outputs — Energy In
/ Out / Net triptych, a day-by-day SOC projection table with green/amber/
red banding, and a verdict naming how many recharge drives a stay needs and
on which days. OWNER-RATIFIED MODEL: stationary days are solar-only (engine
off, so no alternator term); a recharge drive fires ONLY on a day the bank
would otherwise breach the 20% floor — the owner's "drive only when needed"
doctrine, NOT a fixed every-N-days cadence. The SOC trajectory carries no
loss factor, matching the existing single-day maths so the two cannot
disagree.

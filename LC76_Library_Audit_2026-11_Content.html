No critical finding. Five high, fifty-nine medium, sixty-five low. The library is sound in structure — links, anchors, the index and the automatic checks all hold — and most of its content is current. What it suffers from is drift: a fact changes on the vehicle or by your ruling, the owning page is corrected, and copies elsewhere are not. Much of what was found is of that kind.

Three patterns account for most of what was found.
1. Copies that disagree with their owner — the overheating drill three ways (F138, F167), who to ring first (F79), the jacking point (F152), jump-starting (F174), the bleed point (F141).
2. Figures with no source — thermostat, compression, fuel range, fuse ratings, socket sizes, voltage bands. Several came from generic guides or planning drawings and were never traced to this vehicle (F89, F127, F128, F139, F176, F181).
3. Status lines overtaken by the 2026 work — pages still describing the April service, the old shocks, the Maxxis tyres, the Willard battery, the Karoo loop as ahead (F97, F155, F163, F184, F178, F121).

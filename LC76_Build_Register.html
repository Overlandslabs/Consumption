§1How to Read This RegisterDERIVED VIEW · CONVENTIONS

This document is a derived view of the Build_Accessories sheet of LC_Service_history_FINAL.xlsx — the master of fitted kit & acquisition cost. It records capitalised build and accessory items, kept apart from the Services maintenance & repair ledger. The xlsx is always authoritative; this file is regenerated from it and never edited directly.

The insurance valuation is the derived value view of this same sheet — it filters these rows by Fitment type and Status (Permanent vs Removable; excluding Removed-superseded) and is held offline as a broker submission. This register is the source; the valuation is the filter — neither competes with the other.

Basis. Cost is the actual invoice amount (VAT-incl). Replacement is current replacement value, used only where no cost is on record (flagged in Notes). Removed-superseded rows (dimmed below) are kept for history and excluded from insurance value. Of the R563,586.01 fitted value, R456,759.56 is permanent fitment and R106,826.45 removable trip equipment.

Total cost of vehicle. The base vehicle (original purchase, 10 Feb 2010, R394,000.00) is carried as the first row, tagged Fitment type Base vehicle. Base + build & kit = R960,007.01 total invested. The base is insured at agreed value (a separate exercise, R450,000—R550,000 range), NOT this historical purchase cost, so it is excluded from the insurance accessory schedule and from the R566,007.01 build & kit subtotal. Of the R960,007.01, R883,087.01 is invoiced cost (base R394,000 + build/kit at cost R489,087.01) and R76,920.00 is at replacement value where no invoice is on record.

Total cost of ownership — this asset figure plus lifetime maintenance & repair (R816,890.60) — is surfaced in the R8 Service & Cost Dashboard (R1,776,897.61).

Entry shape (normalised 29 Jul 2026, OI-59) — three lines, in this order:
  line 1   OI-n Title (the technical name — codes and cross-references)
  line 2   WHAT: one plain-English line, no codes, readable by ANYONE
  line 3   OWNER · GATE/DEADLINE · SOURCE
  line 4   STATUS — followed by any narrative
  line 5   CONFIRMED <date> (source) · NEXT-DUE: <trigger/cadence/review>
           (added 30 Jul 2026, AoT S5, CC-8 fix — every entry now carries the
           date its status was last confirmed and when it next needs checking;
           STANDING items carry a cadence, WAITING-DATE items their trigger)

THE «WHAT:» LINE IS NOT DECORATION (adopted 29 Jul 2026). The titles in this
register are written in project shorthand — RA-, EX-, TA-, R-numbers, § refs —
which is efficient for the people who wrote them and opaque to everyone else,
including the second crew member on whom RA-8 solo capability depends. Every
entry therefore carries a plain-English line naming the actual job. It is what
the dashboard displays as the heading; the technical title sits under it.
STATUS ALWAYS STANDS ON ITS OWN LINE and never abuts the finding text it
annotates. An annotation prepended to an older sentence inverts that
sentence's meaning — see OI-36, repaired 29 Jul 2026.

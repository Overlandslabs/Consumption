The northern circuit (Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire) and the wilder south (Ruaha, Nyerere, Katavi). Entry from Zambia. Carnet required, eVisa. Diesel around TZS 3,200/L. Compiled May 2026.
Profile 007 · Country
🇲🇿 Southern Mozambique

The coast from Maputo to Beira — entry points, visa-free 30 days (verify the ETA rollout), compulsory SORCA insurance, and the Save–Beira banditry stretch to plan around. Diesel around MZN 116/L, with 2026 supply gaps. Compiled Jul 2026.
Profile 008 · Country
🇲🇼 Malawi

The length of the country along Lake Malawi — entry from Mozambique, visa-free 90 days, COMESA insurance. Some of Africa's dearest diesel and chronic shortages, so don't rely on fuel here. Bilharzia in the lake. Compiled Jul 2026.
Profile 009 · Country
🇿🇼 Northern Zimbabwe

The northern Zambezi — Kariba, Mana Pools and Hwange. Entry from Mozambique, exit to Zambia at Chirundu. USD cash economy, COMESA insurance. Some of Africa's dearest diesel; year-round malaria in the valley. Compiled Jul 2026.
Profile 010 · Country
🇰🇪 Kenya

The northern end of the road — Nairobi, the Mara, Amboseli, Tsavo, Samburu and the coast. Entry from Tanzania, eTA-exempt for SA. M-Pesa essential, high park fees, and the best Toyota network on the route. Compiled Jul 2026.
